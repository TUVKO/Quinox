How Equinox Wallet Works

Equinox Wallet pools client deposits into a professionally managed trading portfolio. Our team allocates capital across currency markets, commodities, and digital assets, using a combination of algorithmic strategies and experienced market analysis to generate consistent daily returns.

When you deposit into a Growth Plan, your funds join this managed pool. A portion of the profit generated from our trading activity is distributed back to you as a fixed 10% daily return, credited automatically to your account for 80 days — a total return of 800% on your original investment.

We manage risk through diversification, disciplined position sizing, and continuous monitoring of market conditions, so returns to our clients remain steady regardless of daily market volatility.

Beyond trading, Equinox Wallet rewards you for growing our community. Share your referral link and earn 10% on deposits from anyone you refer directly, 4% from their referrals, and 2% from the level after that — three tiers of ongoing income for building your network.

Whether you invest UGX 10,000 or UGX 100 million, our commitment is the same: disciplined trading, transparent daily payouts, and reliable access to your funds.
